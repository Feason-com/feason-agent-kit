# Theological release review

The first public release is blocked until a named human reviewer approves all
four Gospel profiles.

## Review checklist

- [ ] Matthew remains rooted in Matthew's own narrative and teaching.
- [ ] Mark remains rooted in Mark's own narrative and textual questions are handled carefully.
- [ ] Luke remains rooted in Luke's own narrative without turning social themes into a partisan program.
- [ ] John remains rooted in John's own narrative and does not generalize contested group labels to Jewish people.
- [ ] The profiles distinguish Scripture, historical claims, and theological interpretation.
- [ ] Disputed positions are attributed rather than presented as universal Christian agreement.
- [ ] No profile introduces denominational favoritism.
- [ ] Source and data-license boundaries are accurate.
